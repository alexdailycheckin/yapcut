# Trend creation: the first-mover lane

Riding trends is defence; creating them is the lean. A trend is a template other people can
perform: it needs a copyable skeleton, low effort and high identity fit, and a NAME (the
theory is section 5 of `virality-psychology.md`). Two pipelines, both live in every weekly
run, and one measurement step that used to be missing.

## 1. Named formats

Each lane keeps one or two named recurring series with a fixed skeleton and a signature
line. If a lane has none, propose ONE candidate per run (skeleton, signature line, episode 1
script) until the creator locks one. A format graduates to a trend when a stranger makes their
own episode.

## 2. Coined terms

Copy `coined-terms-template.md` into the workspace as `coined-terms.md` (the ledger). Keep
one or two candidate terms in play at any time: each names a problem or dynamic the audience
already FEELS but has no word for. Rules: every mention uses the EXACT same term (repetition
is the planting); the term gets its own definition beat in at least one script per week
while in play ("I call this X: it's when..."); the term's page goes up on the creator's site
EARLY, because AI engines cite the coiner and a term with no page cannot be cited.

## 3. The pickup step (between MEASURE and PUBLISH, every run)

For every term in play: one exact-phrase search across the web, LinkedIn and Reddit, and
the result appended to the ledger with the date (`pickup: none | <url>`). A term graduates
when someone else uses it unprompted. A term with no pickup after about four weeks is retired
in the ledger, in those words, so it is not re-proposed. The definition beat in a script is
conditional on the term having a live page: no page, no beat.

Open every weekly digest with a "3 rising signals" block: the sharpest early-detection calls
from the sweep, each as a one-line prediction with its transferred version. Being on record
early is the credibility play even when a call misses.
